# GitHub Copilot Adoption Delivery

This context names the parts of a GitHub Copilot Adoption Delivery Session and
the customization concepts participants use throughout it.

## Language

**Session**:
A time-boxed adoption event in which participants follow a track and produce a
working outcome.
_Avoid_: Workshop, training

**Stage**:
An ordered part of a track that advances one solution through a sequence of
increasingly capable outcomes.
_Avoid_: Phase, step

**Phase**:
An ordered period of work that groups related activities across roles or a
delivery lifecycle.
_Avoid_: Stage, step

**Customization Trio**:
The three complementary customization types: Repository Instructions, Custom
Agents, and Custom Skills.
_Avoid_: Customization stack, customization bundle

**Repository Instructions**:
Shared guidance that gives Copilot the conventions and expectations of a
repository.
_Avoid_: Project rules, repo prompt

**Custom Agent**:
A named Copilot specialist with a defined role and area of responsibility.
_Avoid_: Persona, assistant

**Custom Skill**:
A reusable procedure for handling a specific kind of task.
_Avoid_: Workflow, playbook

**Design Brief**:
A concise statement of the intended outcome, audience, constraints, and quality
bar for a customization.
_Avoid_: Prompt, specification

**Use Cue**:
A recognizable situation or request that indicates when a Custom Agent or
Custom Skill applies.
_Avoid_: Trigger, activation phrase
