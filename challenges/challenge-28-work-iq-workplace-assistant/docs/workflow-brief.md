# Project Handoff Brief

Your engineering group is handing a customer-facing feature to a delivery team.
The context sits across recent meetings, Teams messages, email, and project
documents. The delivery lead needs one reviewed handoff that shows:

- the current delivery decision
- work items with an owner and target date when those facts are present
- open risks and dependencies
- the source item that supports each statement

Do not summarize everything in Microsoft 365. Keep the handoff to one named
project and a recent time period agreed before retrieval starts.

## Constraints

- Retrieve only information the signed-in user is already allowed to access.
- Keep the project scope and time range narrow.
- Separate observed facts from assumptions and gaps.
- Do not send messages, create tasks, modify files, or update calendar items
  until a human reviews the draft.
- Do not include sensitive content in the final artifact unless the delivery
  lead has confirmed that the artifact's location and audience are appropriate.

## Expected Output

Create a Markdown action register and a short delivery summary. Every action,
risk, dependency, and decision needs evidence that a reviewer can open. If an
owner or date is missing, say so. Do not guess.
