# Stakeholder Priorities

The statements below were collected in separate interviews. Some cannot all be satisfied at once.

## Chief Operating Officer

Show customers one order history within 90 days. A read-only first release is acceptable if support can explain freshness and source.

## Chief Financial Officer

The first combined close must reconcile to both source ledgers. Do not hide timing differences behind one total.

## Chief Information Security Officer

Keep seller workforce access separate from retail customer administration. Identity linking must not collapse authorization boundaries.

## Data Protection Officer

No combined marketing until consent purpose, region, and provenance are represented in the shared contract. Account linking alone is not consent.

## Head of Customer Support

Agents need one search, not two browser tabs. If data is stale or unavailable, show that plainly instead of returning an empty result.

## Marketplace General Manager

Do not put seller operations on the retail release calendar. Marketplace order and settlement identifiers must remain stable.

## HPC Commerce Director

Avoid invasive OMS changes before the holiday freeze in five months. A change-data or adapter approach is acceptable during transition.

## Integration Platform Lead

Use managed Azure services, standard observability, and a small contract governance process. The team cannot support a custom broker and a custom API gateway.

## Delivery Sponsor

Every wave needs a business outcome, rollback condition, owner, and measurable exit criterion. Architecture work without a 90-day delivery path will not be funded.
