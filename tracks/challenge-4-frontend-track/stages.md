# Frontend: Stages

## Stages

| Stage | Name | Difficulty | Est. Time | Key Deliverable |
|-------|------|------------|-----------|----------------|
| Setup | [Environment and skill checks](../challenge-4-frontend-track.md#getting-started) | ⭐ | 30 min | Copilot customizations, Impeccable available |
| 1 | [Component Architecture and Layout](stage-1-component-architecture.md) | ⭐ | 60 min | Responsive dashboard, routing, one critique-and-fix cycle |
| 2 | [State Management and CRUD](stage-2-state-management.md) | ⭐⭐ | 60 min | Context + useReducer, validated forms, delete with undo |
| 3 | [Advanced Interactions](stage-3-advanced-interactions.md) | ⭐⭐ | 30 min | Clear task-form feedback and empty states |
| 4 | [Accessibility and Performance](stage-4-accessibility.md) | ⭐⭐⭐ | 45 min | Accessibility bug hunt, axe and keyboard checks |
| 5 | [Integration and Testing](stage-5-integration.md) | ⭐⭐⭐ | 75 min | Mocked API, tested task flow, final polish |

The core takes **five hours including setup**. Allow up to six hours for debugging. Complete the stages in order and use Impeccable at the review points. Participants decide which recommendations to apply and verify that changes preserve the task flow.

> **Four-hour session:** Preflight the environment before the clock starts. Use two routes in Stage 1 and limit the final polish to the task form. Keep CRUD, accessibility checks, and the required critique-and-fix evidence.

Stretch tasks are optional and sit outside the core time budget. They include drag-and-drop Kanban, theme switching, list virtualization, offline support, and Storybook.
