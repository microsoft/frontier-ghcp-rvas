# Merger Integration Architecture: Stages

## Stages

| Stage | Name | Duration | What You Do |
|-------|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | [Map the Merger](stage-1-map.md) | 60-75 minutes | Map capabilities, systems, constraints, authority, and ownership conflicts |
| 2 | [Choose Boundaries and Patterns](stage-2-boundaries.md) | 60-75 minutes | Compare integration patterns and define target boundaries without jumping to services |
| 3 | [Make the Architecture Reviewable](stage-3-architecture.md) | 90-105 minutes | Produce Azure diagrams, a contract, and three or four concise ADRs |
| 4 | [Sequence the Transition](stage-4-roadmap.md) | 60-75 minutes | Define waves, transition states, risks, rollback, and measurable exit criteria |

Each stage narrows the next. Do not start the Azure container diagram before the authority conflicts and target boundaries are visible.

> **Short on time?** Complete the customer identity and order-history path only. Still produce both diagrams, one contract, three ADRs, and a two-wave roadmap. Mark reporting decisions that remain open.
